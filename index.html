    HOW TO PLAY

    STEP 1
    HOLD

    One player holds the device flat on their forehead, screen facing out.

    STEP 2
    DESCRIBE

    Everyone else explains the word — but never says the word itself.

    STEP 3
    GUESS

    The holder listens to the clues and says an answer out loud.

    STEP 4
    TILT

    Tilt down for CORRECT, tilt up to PASS, then back to the middle.

    TAP CONTROL

    MISTAKE?

    Tap the ↺ button at the top of the screen to undo the last word and try it again.

    TEAM BATTLE

    Each team plays one round with the same word list. More correct answers wins.
